SMMC at Monash

The Simon Marais Mathematics Competition is an annual international undergraduate mathematics competition. Students solve "interesting but challenging" mathematics problems and can compete as individuals or in pairs.

Most current undergraduate and Honors students are eligible to enter. The more students enter, the better Monash does as a team, so everyone should consider entering regardless of their level.

The deadline for students to register is 5 September, 2025. The competition itself is on Saturday, 11 October, 2025. To register and/or if you have any questions, please email Andy Hammerlindl at

andy.hammerlindl@monash.edu

More details and past problems are available at the website:
